<h1 align="center">📊 Foresight_PowerBI_Internship <br> Forggith Pharmaceuticals Dashboard</h1>
<p align="center"><em>Internship Project for the Data Science &amp; Business Analytics Program – IIT Guwahati (via Emeritus)</em></p>

-----

## Objective  

Develop a strategic dashboard using **Power BI** to analyze distributor sales data for Forggith Pharmaceuticals. This includes insights into:

- ✅ Revenue trends and YTD performance  
- ✅ Regional and channel-based distribution  
- ✅ Marketing effectiveness by team and representative  
- ✅ Product category performance  
- ✅ Target achievement metrics  

---

## Learning Outcomes  

- Translate distributor sales into **impactful business intelligence**  
- Build performance dashboards tailored for diverse stakeholder needs  
- Apply DAX calculations to support KPI analysis across teams  

---

## 🗂️ Dataset Overview  

### 📦 Distributor Sales Records  
Monthly submissions from distributors capturing:  
- `DistributorID`, `Region`, `Channel`, `ProductClass`, `SalesTeam`, `SalesRep`, `OrderDate`, `UnitsSold`, `RevenueGenerated`, `Target`

---

## Power BI Implementation  

### 🔗 Data Modeling  
- Unified distributor entries into a centralized fact table  
- Integrated product classes and team hierarchy for slicing  
- Time intelligence using `OrderDate` for YTD/SPLY metrics  
  

### DAX Measures  
- `Revenue YTD`, `SPLY Revenue`, `Target YTD`  
- `Month-on-Month % Change`  
- `Target Achievement %`  

---

## Design & Formatting Strategy  

- Applied Forggith’s **corporate palette** for branding consistency  
- Structured report into clear navigational sections  
- Custom tooltips & slicers for executive-level usability
